Plaivo plays video with libmpv (https://mpv.io), Copyright (C) the mpv
developers. libmpv is not part of the Plaivo executable: it is a separate
shared library (libmpv-2.dll on Windows, libmpv.2.dylib on macOS) loaded at
runtime from the folder next to the Plaivo executable. You may replace it with
your own build of libmpv.

libmpv built without GPL-only components (meson option -Dgpl=false), as
shipped with Plaivo, is licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1 or (at your option) any later version.

It contains FFmpeg and the other libraries listed after it, each under its own
license.
